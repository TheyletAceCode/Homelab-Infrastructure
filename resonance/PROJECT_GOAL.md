# Project goal

## Objective

One private music experience for one owner that feels premium on iPhone and in the car, plays
the owner's own files anywhere, and gets smarter from the owner's own listening, without new
servers, subscriptions or heavy AI.

## Must have (in priority order)

1. iPhone playback that is reliable at home and away (Tailscale).
2. Offline downloads that survive no signal.
3. CarPlay and Bluetooth: browse, play, skip, resume after calls, lock-screen controls.
4. Synced lyrics where data exists.
5. Smart playlists and automatic mixes from the owner's own history.
6. Simple import of the owner's files from Windows, Mac and iPhone.
7. Playlist import from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and SoundCloud by matching songs already owned. Never by copying subscription audio.

## Quality bars

- Privacy: listening data stays on the server unless the owner opts in to a public scrobbler.
- Reliability: Resonance never breaks basic Navidrome playback; every addition can be removed.
- Resource budget: all Resonance additions together stay under 300 MB RAM steady state on the 8 GB host.
- Cost: free and open source first; anything paid needs the owner's approval.
- Rosetta integration is optional and never required for playback.

## Non-goals

- A custom iPhone app or a Navidrome replacement.
- Downloading or ripping audio from streaming services.
- Public internet exposure of Navidrome.
